About the software Page 6

## About software:
Unicom software, a full system control designed to help you with all
solution witch can be used with Unicom system to have access and
control on every single movement in your enterprise, it might be good to
know how many and witch product are sold, what time with any
employee and at witch price, it make control easier by connecting the
whole enterprise in one system and auto calculation of quantities, orders,
prices then profit. Starting from creating the inventory files, receiving
data from p.o.s., printing invoices, controlling stock in & out, accounting,
ending by reports, that's what we can call a full system from A to Z.
Who is still doubt, among various control systems, Unicom system takes
up most of the market, because it's easy to use as well as economical and
capable of developing various types of applications. The current software
version is Version 9.0.2 and their main functions are as follows:
* Network
All p.o.s. are connected to the system by the network so that you
can send/receive data, and show you every thing, now! You can change
prices, check sales, sitting in your office.
* Inventory
First of all, organize your company, you can add many department,
create categories and other basics, now you will have to create your
database by adding your inventory to the system, at the same time fix
quantities, prices, cost etc... And send it to the p.o.s.
After receiving data you can check invoices and reports and make orders.
* Accounting
Accounting will help you to manage relations between your
enterprise and the suppliers as well with the whole group of employees,
check transactions, sales, payment and controlling all financial balances.

System presentation Page 7

* Customer File
For special or permanent customers, loyalty cards , discount cards ,
points , cash back , credit sales …
* Employee/Payroll
Keep your employees under control, watch their movements and calculate
salaries.
** Operation procedure steps as follow:
1. System installation: will be installed by allowed trained person.
2. Connection: ask for technician to install the network system.
3. Create: locations, departments, categories, suppliers, cashier etc...
4. Create: inventory data base with the necessary information.
5. Create: employees and customers personnel files.
6. Send: data to p.o.s. and start working!
7. Be the manager: take your time to be familiar with the system by
editing your database.
